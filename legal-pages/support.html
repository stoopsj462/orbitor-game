Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Tap anywhere to make your ship leap between the two orbit rings. Thread the gaps in the barriers and pick up stars. The game speeds up the longer you last.
How do I unlock the orb colors?
New orb colors unlock automatically as your best score passes each threshold shown on the menu. They can't be bought.
What are stars for?
Stars you collect during a run are banked and spent in the Star Shop on ship trails and planet themes. There are no in-app purchases — everything is earned by playing.
Is there any way to lose my progress?
Your best score, stars, and cosmetics are stored on your device. Deleting the app clears them, and there is no cloud backup, so they don't transfer to a new device.
Does Orbitor need the internet?
No. It works fully offline and makes no network connections.
Does Orbitor collect any data?
No. See our Privacy Policy.
