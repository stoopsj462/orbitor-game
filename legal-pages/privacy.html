Orbitor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Orbitor ("the app," "we," "us") is a one-tap arcade game. We built it to collect nothing about you. This policy explains what the app stores and the choices you have. By using Orbitor you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • Orbitor has no accounts of its own, no logins, and no servers.
  • It works fully offline. The only online feature is Apple Game Center leaderboards, which are optional.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you or your device.

Data stored on your device

Orbitor saves your best score, your banked stars, and your cosmetic choices (orb color, ship trail, and planet theme) locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Data we collect

None. Orbitor does not gather, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers.

Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Orbitor submits your best score for each difficulty level to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and shows those leaderboards in the app. This is handled entirely by Apple under your Game Center account and Apple's Privacy Policy; we never receive your Game Center identity or any other information about you. You can turn Game Center off at any time in your device's Settings.

Children

Orbitor is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the revised version at this URL.
