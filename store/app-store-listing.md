## Description (4000)
Leap between orbits. Don't crash.

Orbitor is a one-tap arcade game about timing and nerve. Your glowing ship circles a distant planet on two rings — tap to jump between them, thread the barriers, and grab every star before the whole thing speeds up and pulls you under.

One more run. Every time.

FEATURES
• One-tap controls — instantly playable, endlessly hard to master
• Pure neon style — every pixel drawn in code: glowing trails, particle bursts, screen shake
• 9 unlockable orb colors — earned by pushing your high score, from Aurora to the legendary Void
• A Star Shop — spend the stars you collect on 5 ship trails (Comet, Sparkle, Ribbon, Pulse, Rainbow) and 5 planet themes
• Two goals at once — score for prestige colors, stars for cosmetics
• Play anywhere — iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with your progress saved

No ads. No in-app purchases. No tracking. Pay once, orbit forever.

How far can you go before gravity wins?
